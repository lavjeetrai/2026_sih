# Oil India Limited (OIL) · HSE SIF-Precursor & Life-Saving Rules Platform

An AI/NLP-powered Health, Safety & Environment (HSE) intelligence dashboard for **Oil India Limited (OIL)** that ranks installations/activities by **SIF-Precursor (Serious Injury & Fatality Precursor) Density** and auto-maps observations to **IOGP Life-Saving Rules (LSR)**, empowering safety leadership to focus interventions where fatal potential is highest.

---

## 🎯 Key Capabilities

- **SIF-Precursor Density Ranking**: Ranks drilling rigs, group gathering stations (GGS), pipelines, and workshops by SIF precursor concentration rate.
- **IOGP Life-Saving Rules Mapping**: Analyzes Unsafe Act / Unsafe Condition (UA/UC) reports and auto-maps infractions to the 9 Life-Saving Rules (Line of Fire, Working at Height, Energy Isolation, Safe Mechanical Lifting, Confined Space, Hot Work, etc.).
- **Live AI Safety Assistant**: Instant NLP classification of field observations and near-miss logs with fatal potential risk rating and recommended interventions.
- **HSE Intervention & CAPA Board**: Converts high SIF-P observations into trackable field actions, audits, and supervisor sign-offs.
