AI/NLP Engine to Detect Serious Injury & Fatality (SIF) Precursors

1. Ingest OIL free-text safety reports:
   - Unsafe Act / Unsafe Condition
   - Near Miss
   - Incident reports

   - SIF-potential

   - activity
   - location
   - safety protection / barrier failure

5. Provide an interactive dashboard.

6. Rank sites and activities by SIF-related warning/precursor priority.

7. Help HSE teams focus interventions where serious/fatal potential
   is highest.
